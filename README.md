# Chalo Campus

A sample website for a college-only app where students create, share and join plans around campus: chai, football, a café, a movie, a walk. No official event needed.

This is a design preview. The plans and people on the page are made up, and nothing is saved.

## What the page covers

- **Post a plan:** type, time, public venue picker, group size (3 or more people), cost, women-only and host-approval toggles.
- **Plan feed:** filters, see who's going, join or ask to join, report a plan.
- **Safety:** verified students only, public places, share-my-plan, SOS, check-ins, private ratings, report and block.
- **Bookings:** everyone pays their share into hold, released once the plan is confirmed, with a small example fee calculator.
- **Habit loops:** weekly repeat plans, interests, people you've met.
- **Launch plan:** one campus at a time, with a waitlist form and a venue-partner card.

## Files

- `design/Main.dc.html`: the page, written as a Claude Design canvas artboard.
- `design/canvas.json`: the canvas layout file that goes with it.

These two files are in the Claude Design canvas format, so they open in Claude's Design canvas and not directly in a browser.
